New to Twitch? These are the terms you'll meet in HighBazar and on Twitch itself. The same explanations appear in the app when you point at a small ⓘ.

## People and roles

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Streamer / broadcaster** | You – the owner of the channel. |
| **Viewer / chatter** | Someone watching; a chatter also writes in chat. |
| **Lurker** | Someone who watches without writing. Most viewers are lurkers – a good bot invites them to chat. |
| **Follower** | Someone who clicked *Follow* to be notified when you go live. Free. |
| **Subscriber (sub)** | Someone who pays monthly to support you (Prime Gaming gives one free sub a month). Subs get a badge and your channel's emotes. Tiers 1, 2 and 3 cost more each. |
| **Moderator (mod)** | A trusted viewer who can delete messages, time out and ban in your chat. Give the role only to people you know well (`/mod name`). |
| **VIP** | A trusted viewer with a diamond badge who may skip slow and follower-only mode – a thank-you without moderation rights. |
| **Editor** | Someone you allowed to change your title and category or run ads for you. |
| **Affiliate / Partner** | Twitch's monetization levels. Affiliates and partners get subs, Bits, channel points, polls, predictions and ads. Partners get more, e.g. a stream delay. |

## Support and events

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bits / cheer** | Twitch's own currency. A cheer is a tip with Bits in a chat message; 100 Bits are about one US dollar for you. |
| **Gift sub** | Someone buys a subscription for another viewer or for the community. |
| **Resub** | A subscriber shares how long they've been subscribed. |
| **Raid** | Another streamer ends their stream and sends their viewers to you. Thank the raider – a shoutout is common. |
| **Shoutout** | Twitch's official shoutout: a card in your chat that promotes another streamer with a Follow button. Only while live, at most every 2 minutes. |
| **Hype Train** | When many people subscribe or cheer within a short time, chat works together to reach higher levels and unlock reward emotes. |
| **Watch streak** | Someone watched several of your streams in a row – Twitch lets them share it in chat. |
| **Channel points** | Free, virtual points viewers earn by watching and chatting, spent on rewards you create (“Hydrate!”, “Ask the bot”). Affiliates and partners only. |
| **Poll** | Viewers vote between up to 5 options in a box above chat. |
| **Prediction** | Viewers bet channel points on an outcome; the winners share the losers' points. |

## Chat

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Emote** | A small picture in chat, written as a word (`Kappa`, `catJAM`). Twitch has its own; your subscribers get your channel's emotes. |
| **BTTV, FFZ, 7TV** | BetterTTV, FrankerFaceZ and 7TV – free browser extensions that add more emotes. Viewers only see them as images if they use the extension. |
| **Zero-width emote** | An emote drawn on top of the previous one, e.g. a hat or rain (7TV, BTTV). |
| **First-time chatter** | Someone writing in your chat for the first time – Twitch marks them, and HighBazar can welcome them. |
| **Reply** | Twitch lets you answer a specific message (point at it → Reply). |
| **Announcement** | A highlighted message with a colored bar, for important things. |
| **Pinned message** | A message pinned at the top of chat so everyone sees it. |
| **Shared chat** {#shared-chat} | Several streamers streaming together can merge their chats; messages from the partner channels appear in yours. HighBazar shows them but doesn't moderate or welcome them. |
| **Backseating** | Telling the streamer how to play when they didn't ask – many channels have a rule against it. |
| **Spoilers** | Revealing story twists – another common chat rule. |

## Chat modes and safety

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Emote-only** | Only messages made of emotes are allowed. |
| **Followers-only** | Only people who have followed for at least a set time can write – very effective against bot and hate raids. |
| **Subscribers-only** | Only subscribers can write. |
| **Slow mode** | Each person can send one message every few seconds. |
| **Unique chat (R9K)** | A message that repeats a recent one is blocked. |
| **Chat delay** | Messages of normal viewers appear a few seconds later, so mods can step in. |
| **Timeout** | Mutes someone for a while – they can still watch. |
| **Ban** | Removes someone from your chat until you unban them. They can send an *unban request*. |
| **Warn** | A warning the person must accept before chatting again. |
| **AutoMod** | Twitch's own filter: it holds risky messages back until a moderator allows or denies them. Levels 0–4. |
| **Blocked terms** | Words Twitch (or HighBazar) always blocks in your chat. |
| **Shield mode** | An emergency button that switches on your strictest chat settings at once. |
| **Hate raid** | Many (often fake) accounts spamming hate at once. Shield mode and followers-only mode help. |
| **Suspicious user** | An account you flagged: *Monitor* highlights its messages for your mods, *Restrict* hides them from everyone else. |

## Stream

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Category** | The game or topic of your stream (e.g. *Just Chatting*). Viewers browse Twitch by category. |
| **Tags** | Keywords viewers can filter by in the directory. |
| **Content classification labels** | Warnings for mature content such as violence, drugs, gambling or sexual themes. |
| **Branded content** | Paid promotion or sponsorship in your stream – Twitch shows viewers a notice. |
| **VOD** | The recording of your past stream. |
| **Clip** | A short video of a moment of your stream that viewers can share. |
| **Stream marker** | A bookmark in your VOD to find a moment again. |
| **Stream delay** | Delays your stream for viewers, e.g. against stream sniping. Partners only. |
| **Stream sniping** | Opponents in a competitive game watching your stream to find you. |
| **Pre-roll / mid-roll** | Ads before the stream starts for a new viewer / during your stream. After a mid-roll, Twitch skips pre-rolls for a while. |
| **Ads Manager** | Twitch's settings for ad density and automatic ads. |
| **Schedule** | Your stream schedule on your channel page – viewers can get reminded. |
| **Prefix** | HighBazar's marker (e.g. `[HighBazar]`) in front of bot messages sent from your account, so viewers know they come from the bot. |
